Auto detailing

Price menu

Pick interior, exterior or both, in Basic or Select. Book a full detail and save compared with booking them separately.

SUVs are 15% more than sedans. Trucks are 10% more than SUVs.

Basic

  • Interior: vacuum, wipe down all surfaces, windows, door jambs
  • Exterior: hand wash, wheels and tires, dry, tire shine

Select

  • Interior: everything in Basic, plus carpet and seat shampoo, steam clean, leather clean and condition
  • Exterior: everything in Basic, plus iron decontamination, clay bar, paint sealant
  • Unlocks the monthly Select membership

Paint polish

Removes swirls and light scratches. Available with a Select Exterior or Select Full Detail, since those include the iron decontamination and clay bar prep.

Deeper correction is quoted from photos of your paint, which you can add when you book.

Ceramic coating

Long-term gloss and protection. Available with a Select Exterior or Select Full Detail. We recommend a polish first to restore clarity in the paint before it's coated.
